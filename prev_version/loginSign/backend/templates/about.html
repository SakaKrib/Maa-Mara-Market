ABOUT US

Maa Mara was founded in 2024 by William Okoth Opondo. William built an ecomerce website and named it Maa - the word originating from a popular clan in Nairobi 'the Maasai Mara', 'maa' 'mara'. He built the website to help the people in the community, to make income out of art and craft talent. Products here are authentic and antique. some of them were used in the past to perform cultural activities such as wedding. for instance, the Maasai weding head gear with was worn on the head to indicate royalty and also it symbolized integrity.
